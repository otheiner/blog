About

Hello stranger,

my name is Ondřej, friends call me Ondra and this is my personal blog. Some people might ask why am I creating blog in the time of social media but my answer is simple. We all can probably agree that almost nobody reads long posts on Facebook or Instagram, so I decided to create this blog where I can write long posts that nobody reads. I am not intending to post things regularly but I might have something to say from time to time.

For those who would prefer listening to the articles, I am also creating AI narated versions of my posts and you can subscribe to them through your podscast app via RSS feed. Narrated versions are missing pictures, but you can listen to them while you are on your own adventure and then check pictures later.

If you would like to know more about who I am professionally, you can go to my personal webpage.
